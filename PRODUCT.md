# NAR — product truth

NAR helps people in El Salvador share seats on trips drivers already plan to make. It is carpooling, not on-demand taxi service or a company fleet.

The first usable local beta must let a person create one account, publish a future trip with ordered stops, find an exact compatible stop segment, request seats, and let the driver accept or reject the request. Acceptance must never exceed capacity. A person can be both driver and passenger.

Spanish for El Salvador, USD, and `America/El_Salvador` are the defaults. The brand logo and existing primary/secondary palette are authoritative. Identity checks, payments, ratings, route geometry, live tracking, email and notification delivery are not operational. The legacy prototype remains explicitly labeled as a demo.

The core action is finding an existing trip. The UI should prioritize origin, destination, date, seats and a clear next step. Trust comes from truthful route, stop, driver and request status information, never invented badges or statistics.
