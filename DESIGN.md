# NAR — visual system for the local beta

Keep the verified NAR logo, Inter/Manrope, and the existing dark background with green `#00c29a` and blue `#2563eb` accents. Use light surfaces selectively for the primary search form. The route line and ordered stops are the recurring visual motif.

The beta prioritizes readable content, generous touch targets and clear request states. A trip is presented as one coherent row with time, origin, stops, destination, seats and contribution. No fictional portrait, verification badge, rating or network-wide metric appears in the functional area.

Motion is limited to feedback on controls and dialogs. The first viewport must render without WebGL or continuous effects. The legacy demo retains its own illustrative presentation and is reached through an explicitly named demo link.
